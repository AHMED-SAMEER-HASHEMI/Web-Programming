Library

The Riphah University Library provides students and faculty with books, journals, digital resources and research materials. It supports students in their academic and research activities.

Students can use the library to read books, search for academic resources and access digital learning materials.

Library Services

  • Book borrowing and returning
  • Online journals and databases
  • Research and reference services
  • Digital learning resources
  • Photocopying and scanning
  • Wi-Fi and computer facilities

Recommended Books

  • Introduction to Algorithms - Thomas H. Cormen
  • Computer Networks - Andrew S. Tanenbaum
  • Database System Concepts - Abraham Silberschatz
  • Principles of Marketing - Philip Kotler
  • English Grammar in Use - Raymond Murphy

Digital Resources

Students can also access online books, research papers, journals and other digital resources through the university's Information Services Department.
