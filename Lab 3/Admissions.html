Admissions

Welcome to the Admissions section of Riphah International University. We welcome students who are interested in pursuing higher education and developing their academic and professional skills.

Students can apply for different undergraduate programs according to their educational qualifications and eligibility requirements.

Available Programs

Program Eligibility Application Deadline Fee

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission application form
  • Copy of educational certificates
  • Copy of CNIC or B-Form
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Required application fee

Admission Process

  1. Select your desired program.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Complete the admission application form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Wait for the admission confirmation.

Important Information

Applicants are advised to submit their applications before the deadline. Incomplete applications may not be considered.

Students should regularly check the university website and admission notices for important updates.
