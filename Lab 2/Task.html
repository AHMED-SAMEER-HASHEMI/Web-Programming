📖 About the Library

The Riphah International University Library is dedicated to fostering academic excellence, innovation, and value-based learning. The library provides students with books, journals, and digital resources for their studies and research.

🏫 Sections & Services

Available Facilities

  • Digital Learning Resource Center: Research databases and digital resources.
  • Circulation Counter: Book issue, renewal, and return services.
  • Reference Section: Dictionaries, journals, and reference books.
  • Plagiarism Screening Desk: Assistance with research and thesis checking.

📝 Step-by-Step Checkout Process

  1. Search for the required book in the library catalog.
  2. Find the book using its shelf number.
  3. Take the book and your valid student card to the circulation desk.
  4. Collect the book with its due return date.

📌 Borrowing Guidelines

  • Undergraduate Quota: Students can borrow up to 4 textbooks for 14 days.
  • Late Penalties: A fine of Rs. 10 per day is charged for late returns.
  • Restricted Media: Rare manuscripts, dissertations, and project reports cannot be borrowed.
