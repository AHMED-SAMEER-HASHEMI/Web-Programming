Welcome to Riphah Library

A place dedicated to learning, research, and knowledge.

📖 About Riphah Library

Riphah Library provides students with a wide range of books, research materials, and digital resources. It offers a quiet environment for study and learning.

🏫 Library Facilities

  • Textbooks and reference books
  • Digital library resources
  • Reading area
  • Internet access
  • Research materials
  • Quiet study environment

📝 How to Use the Library

  1. Enter the library with your student ID.
  2. Search for the required book.
  3. Check the availability of the book.
  4. Issue the book from the library counter.
  5. Return the book on time.

📌 Library Rules

  • Maintain silence inside the library.
  • Keep the books clean and safe.
  • Do not damage library property.
  • Return books before the due date.
